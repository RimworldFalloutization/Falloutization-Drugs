# Falloutization: Drugs

## Changes

- Beggars ask for caps instead of silver
- Pawns that spawn with combat-enhancing drugs will now get Fallout chems instead of Yayo or Go-Juice
- Ghoul fertility gene now affects Fallout chems
- Khan raid loot will now contain Fallout chems instead of vanilla drugs
- Prerequisite for Medicine Production research project is now FCP Medicinal Drugs
- Vanilla industrial Medicine moved to FCP Chemistry Workbench
- FCP Chemistry Workbench now gets bonus from being in Laboratory room
- Vanilla Furniture Expanded Drug Cabinet links to FCP Chemistry Workbench
- Glitterworld Medicine renamed to Pre-War Medicine

## Replacements

- Penoxycyline => Stimpak in backstory and trait possessions
- Penoxycyline => Radaway when beggers ask for it
- Flake => Jet
- Psychite Tea => Smokeleaf Joint
- Go-Juice => Jet
- Wake-Up => Mentats

## Removals

- All vanilla drugs except Smokeleaf Joint, and their required research projects
- Drug Production research project and Drug Lab building
- Vanilla Furniture Expanded Electric Drug Lab
- Penoxycyline
- VFE Medieval 2 Extract brewing (alchemy): research, extract labs, draughts/extracts, and alchemy plants/ingredients
